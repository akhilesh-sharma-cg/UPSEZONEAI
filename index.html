    ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    Platform Guide & Transparency

    Quick answers about features, privacy, and saved progress.

    01. How does Aspirora AI save my syllabus progress, daily tasks, and notes?
    −

    Your UPSC syllabus checkboxes, daily study tasks, Mains answer drafts, and Current Affairs notes are saved automatically in your browser's localStorage. Your progress persists across page reloads without requiring a login.

    02. How does the Mains Answer Evaluator score my practice answers?
    +

    The front-end evaluator checks your response for four core UPSC rubric pillars: word count adherence, Constitutional Articles/Sections, Supreme Court precedents, and Commission/Committee recommendations (e.g., Sarkaria, Punchhi, 2nd ARC). In a production deployment, this module connects to a backend LLM API for deeper semantic grading.

    03. Can I read the book revision notes right now?
    +

    Select a Polity, Economy, History, or Ethics card to open its revision sheet.

    04. Will my saved study progress appear on another device?
    +

    Progress stays in this browser and may not sync across devices. Clearing site data may erase it.

    05. Can I make a daily UPSC study plan with the planner?
    +

    Yes. Use the study planner to organise your targets, break preparation into manageable tasks, and review your progress regularly. Choose realistic daily goals and adjust them as your schedule changes.

    06. Can the Mains Evaluator guarantee my actual UPSC marks?
    +

    No. Practice feedback is intended to help you review structure, relevance, word count, and supporting examples. Actual marks depend on the examiner and official evaluation process, so treat any practice score as guidance rather than a prediction.

    07. How should I use mock tests effectively?
    +

    Attempt a test under timed conditions, review every incorrect or guessed answer, and note the topics that need revision. Use the results to guide your next study session instead of focusing only on the score.

    08. What are the mind maps useful for?
    +

    Mind maps help you connect related concepts and revise a topic at a glance. Use them alongside standard books, class notes, and previous-year questions; they are a revision aid, not a replacement for detailed study.

    09. Do the revision sheets replace standard UPSC books?
    +

    No. The revision sheets are designed for quick review of selected themes. Build your understanding with standard sources and official material, then use concise notes to revise key points.

    10. Is the ₹99 Library Pass required to use every feature?
    +

    The page labels the Reference Library as a ₹99 Library Pass. Check the current unlock screen and terms before making any payment. Do not assume that this pass is required for features outside the library.

    11. What happens if I clear my browser data?
    +

    Clearing site data or local storage can remove saved tasks, notes, and progress from this browser. If your information is important, keep a separate backup of your notes before clearing browser data.

    12. Are Current Affairs notes enough for the whole exam?
    +

    Current Affairs notes are only one part of preparation. Connect important developments to the UPSC syllabus, revise static subjects, practise previous-year questions, and verify time-sensitive facts through reliable sources such as PIB and official publications.

    13. Can I use Aspirora AI on a mobile phone?
    +

    The page includes responsive styling intended to adapt to smaller screens. For the best experience, use an updated browser. If a control or section looks crowded on your device, try rotating the screen or opening the page on a wider display.

    14. Is Aspirora AI officially connected to UPSC?
    +

    No. Aspirora AI is an independent educational platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). For exam notifications, eligibility, and official instructions, always consult the UPSC website.

    15. Where should I check official exam updates?
    +

    Use the official UPSC website for examination notices and instructions. For current affairs, cross-check important information against reliable primary sources, including PIB releases and relevant government publications.
